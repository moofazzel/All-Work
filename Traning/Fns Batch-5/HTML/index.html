FAQ

Details, policies, and prep.

Transparent information mirrors high-performing med spa sites and builds trust before guests book.

Do consultations include a treatment plan?

Yes. Your provider creates a photographed facial map, outlines recommended treatments, and sends digital aftercare within 24 hours.

What brands and devices do you use?

We carry FDA-cleared lasers, HA and biostimulatory fillers, neuromodulators, and physician-dispensed skincare selected for your skin type.

Is financing available?

Yes. We partner with Cherry and CareCredit for flexible payment options on packages and memberships.

How do I prepare for injectables?

Avoid blood thinners for 48 hours when safe to do so, arrive makeup-free, and plan for minimal swelling for 24–48 hours.
